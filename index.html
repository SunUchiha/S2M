03 / Scanning guidelines

READ BEFORE FIRST SCAN
01
Use soft, even lighting.Avoid harsh reflections, flickering lights, and moving shadows that confuse feature matching.
02
Keep the whole object visible.Leave space around its outline. Do not cut off the top, bottom, or sides of the object.
03
Move slowly and smoothly.Keep neighboring views overlapping by at least 50%. Sudden motion causes blur and dropped frames.
04
Capture multiple viewpoints.Sweep around the object at one height, then repeat at higher and lower angles when practical.
05
Keep the object sharp.Avoid motion blur. Maintain a reasonably consistent distance from the object throughout the scan.
06
Favor textured, matte surfaces.Plain, reflective, or transparent objects are harder for feature-matching reconstruction.
07
Capture complete coverage.Include all visible sides, the top, and any accessible surfaces of the object.
08
Stop when you have enough.A typical scan needs 30 to 100 sharp, overlapping frames. Too few frames reduces reconstruction quality.

06 / Reconstruction

PHOTGRAMMETRY PIPELINE
Reconstruction engine not connected S2M prepares your capture data locally. A separate reconstruction backend is required to generate real 3D geometry. No fake or sample mesh is produced.
01
Image validationCheck sharpness, exposure, and overlap before submission. S2M already computes a Laplacian-based sharpness score per sampled frame.
02
Feature extraction and matchingSIFT or similar features are detected and matched across image pairs to find corresponding points.
03
Structure from MotionEstimate camera positions and orientations from matched features. Produces a sparse point cloud.
04
Dense reconstructionBuild a dense point cloud or multi-view depth map from aligned cameras.
05
Mesh and textureReconstruct a surface mesh, generate UV maps, and project textures from the original images.
06
ExportDeliver a GLB, OBJ, or PLY file ready for the 3D viewer above.
S2M does not invent geometry. Until a backend is connected, no reconstruction is performed and no fake model is generated. See the deployment notes below this code for the recommended COLMAP-based backend architecture.
