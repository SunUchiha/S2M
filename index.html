04 / Scanning guidelines

READ BEFORE FIRST SCAN
01
Use soft, even lighting.Avoid harsh reflections, flickering lights, and moving shadows that confuse feature matching.
02
Keep the whole object visible.Leave space around its outline. Do not cut off the top, bottom, or sides of the object.
03
Move slowly and smoothly.Keep neighboring views overlapping by at least 50%. Sudden motion causes blur and dropped frames.
04
Capture multiple viewpoints.Sweep around the object at one height, then repeat at higher and lower angles when practical.
05
Keep the object sharp.Avoid motion blur. Maintain a reasonably consistent distance from the object throughout the scan.
06
Favor textured, matte surfaces.Plain, reflective, or transparent objects are harder for feature-matching reconstruction.
07
Use the selection tool.Draw a tight shape around your object so the depth-based reconstruction does not include the background.
08
Stop when you have enough.A typical scan needs 30 to 100 sharp, overlapping frames. Pick the sharpest one in the project modal to reconstruct.

07 / Reconstruction

DEPTH-BASED MESH · HONEST STATUS
In-browser depth reconstruction ready S2M runs Depth Anything (small) in your browser to convert a captured frame into a low-poly displaced mesh. The model is downloaded on first use (~25 MB). True multi-view photogrammetry still requires the separate COLMAP backend.
01
Frame selectionOpen a project, click a frame thumbnail to mark it as the source. The selection stored with that frame masks out the background.
02
Depth estimationDepth Anything (small) predicts a per-pixel depth map of the selected frame. Closer surfaces are displaced more in the final mesh.
03
Mesh assemblyA 48x48 vertex grid is displaced by depth. Vertices outside the selection mask are dropped. Original pixel colors become vertex colors.
04
View and exportThe mesh loads into the 3D viewer above. Use Export GLB to save a binary glTF file you can open in Blender, three.js, or any 3D tool.
Honest limitation: this produces a 2.5D relief of the visible side of the object, not a complete 3D scan with back and underside. For full 360-degree reconstruction, the COLMAP backend (planned) is required. No fake geometry is ever generated.
